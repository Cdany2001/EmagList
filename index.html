List prices come from Listă produse – Preț BF Promo (cu TVA). eMag prices are read from eMag's own search results when a card reaches the screen, and kept for a day.

A page on github.io is not allowed to read emag.ro — the browser fetches the reply and then refuses to hand it over, and eMag sends nothing that waives that. Installing tools/emaglist.user.js in Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey lets this page read eMag on your own device. Without it the cards say so instead of showing a price.
